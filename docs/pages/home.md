# Home (Dashboard)

## What this page is

**Home** serves two different purposes depending on whether you're signed in.

### When you're signed out

The page becomes a public landing page that:

- Explains what the app is for
- Shows a sign-in form so you can log in without navigating elsewhere

Once you sign in from the landing page, you're taken straight to the Home dashboard.

### When you're signed in

The page becomes your personal **financial dashboard** — a quick read on where things stand right now.

---

## Dashboard snapshot (three cards)

At the top you'll see three snapshot cards:

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Today's balance** | Your current running balance as of today |
| **Upcoming bills (7 days)** | Total of bills due soon or already past due |
| **Safe-to-spend (Lowest bal)** | The lowest projected balance over the next 6 months, and the date it occurs |

The **Safe-to-spend** number is the most important one. If it's positive, you have breathing room. If it's red (negative), something needs attention before that date arrives.

---

## Spending in [current month]

Below the snapshot cards, the dashboard shows a breakdown of your spending this month by category.

Each category tile shows:

- **Category name**
- **Amount spent** so far this month
- If a budget item exists for that category, the tile also shows the **budgeted monthly amount** so you can compare at a glance
- A **progress bar** indicating how your spending compares to the highest category

A **★** star icon means you're right on budget for that category (within $1 of the budgeted amount).

If you have many categories, the list starts collapsed. A **Show more** button reveals the rest.

---

## Recommended first stop (new users)

If you're setting up the app for the first time:

1. **Budget Items** — create your recurring bills and income
2. **Checkbook** — enter day-to-day transactions and keep the running balance accurate
3. **Budget Forecast** — use it when you want to see what's coming up
4. **Accounts** — keep your account list current so balances stay meaningful

Once those are set up, the Home dashboard becomes a useful daily check-in.
