# Accounts

## What this page is

The **Accounts** page is where you keep track of your financial accounts in one place.

It’s designed to give you a clean, sortable overview of what you have and what’s going on with each account, without turning it into a spreadsheet nightmare.

## What you’ll see

A list of accounts with helpful columns such as:

- **Account Name**
- **Type** (shown on medium+ screens)
- **Balance**
- Optional details (depending on your screen size):
  - **Minimum Payment**
  - **Interest Rate**
  - **Due Day** (entered when editing)
  - **Account Number**
  - **Last Updated**
  - **Ratio**

You can sort and filter the list, and you can also use the **Search** box to quickly find what you need.

## Add an account

1. Select **Add Account**.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Name**
   - **Account #** (optional, but handy)
   - **Type** (choose from the list)
   - **Balance**
   - **Credit Limit** (if applicable)
   - **Available**
   - **Due Day** (day of month)
   - **Min Payment**
   - **Interest**
   - **Web URL** (a shortcut link to the provider)
3. Select **Save**.

Select **Cancel** to leave without saving.

## Edit or delete an account

- Select the **Edit** button next to the account.
- When editing an existing account, you’ll see **Delete this Account**.

Tip: If you’re not sure you want to delete, consider setting the balance to zero or updating the name (for example, “(Closed)”) so you keep history without cluttering your active list.

## Searching and filtering

Use the search bar above the grid to filter by:

- account name
- type
- partial account number (if you store one)

## Why this page helps

When your accounts are up to date, the rest of your budgeting becomes less stressful.

You get quick answers to:

- “How much is left on that card?”
- “What’s the minimum I have to pay this month?”
- “Where do I log in to check details?”

It’s a small page that quietly does a lot of heavy lifting.
