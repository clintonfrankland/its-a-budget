# Budget Forecast

## What this page is

**Budget Forecast** is where the app turns your budget into a forward-looking plan.

Instead of only telling you what happened, this page helps you answer:

- “If I keep going the way I’m going, where will my balance land?”
- “Do I have a tight spot coming up next week?”
- “What happens if that bill is late, or if income arrives early?”

That’s the point of forecasting: fewer surprises, more control.

## What you’ll see

### 1) Balance chart (when you have budget items)

A line chart shows your projected **Balance over time**.

It’s a quick, at-a-glance way to spot:

- upcoming dips
- recovery days (like paychecks)
- the general “shape” of your cash flow

### 2) Budget Forecast list

A list of upcoming budget entries with helpful details like:

- **Due Date**
- **Budget Name**
- **Amount**
- optional info like **Category**, **Frequency**, and **Balance** (depending on screen size)

Icons can appear for certain items:

- Receipt icon: a bill
- Schedule icon: an auto-pay item

## Common actions

### Add a budget item

1. Select **Add Budget**.
2. Fill out:
   - **Name**
   - **Type**: Expense or Income
   - **Amount**
   - **Due Date** (the next due date)
   - **Frequency** (how often it repeats)
   - **Category**
   - Optional bill details:
     - **Is Bill**
     - **Payee** (if it’s a bill)
     - Options like **Auto** and **Late**
   - Optional **End Date** (if the item stops at a certain point)
3. Select **Save**.

### Edit, Mark Paid, or Edit Next

Each row supports quick actions:

- **Edit**: change the overall budget item.
- **Mark Paid**: mark the current occurrence as handled.
- **Edit Next**: adjust the next occurrence without changing the long-term pattern.

“Edit Next” is perfect for real life, where bills are *usually* the same but not always. It helps you stay accurate without breaking your recurring setup.

## Searching and filtering

Use the **Search** box at the top of the list to narrow results.

This is helpful when your list gets big and you just want to find:

- “electric”
- “mortgage”
- “paycheck”

## Why people love this page

Forecasting is the difference between reacting and planning.

When this page is up to date, you can make decisions with confidence because you can actually see the road ahead.
