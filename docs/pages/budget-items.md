# Budget Items

## What this page is

**Budget Items** is your master list of repeating money events.

Think of it like your financial “schedule”:

- regular bills
- recurring income
- subscriptions
- anything that happens on a predictable cycle

Keeping this page accurate is what makes the rest of the app feel effortless.

## What you’ll see

A searchable list with columns like:

- **Budget Name**
- **Category** (may be hidden on small screens)
- **Due Date** (may be hidden on some screen sizes)
- **End Date** (only shown on larger screens)
- **Frequency**
- **Amount**
- **Monthly** (a helpful monthly equivalent)

At the bottom of the **Monthly** column, the app totals the visible monthly amounts. That makes it easy to sanity-check how much of your month is already spoken for.

## Add a budget item

1. Select **Add Budget**.
2. Enter the key details:
   - **Name**
   - **Type**: Expense or Income
   - **Amount**
   - **Due Date**
   - **Frequency**
   - **Category**
3. If it’s a bill, turn on **Is Bill**.
   - You can then pick a **Payee**.
   - You can also flag it as **Auto** (auto-pay) or **Late**.
4. If the item ends (like a temporary payment plan), turn on **Has End Date** and pick an **End Date**.
5. Select **Save**.

## Edit or delete a budget item

- Select **Edit** (pencil icon) next to the item you want to change.
- When editing an existing item, you’ll also have **Delete this Budget**.

Tip: If you only need to change a single upcoming occurrence (just this month’s bill is different), you may prefer doing that from **Budget Forecast** using **Edit Next**, so your recurring setup stays clean.

## Search and filters

Use the **Search** box to quickly narrow down the list.

Common searches:

- a payee name (like “Verizon”)
- a category (like “Utilities”)
- part of a bill name (like “rent”)

## Why this page is a big deal

Once your Budget Items list is solid, the app becomes a system that works *with you*:

- Forecasts become meaningful.
- Checkbook suggestions become smarter.
- You spend less time remembering dates and more time making decisions.
