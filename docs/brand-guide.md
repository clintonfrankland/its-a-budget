# It's a Budget Brand Guide

## Name

- Display name: **It's a Budget**
- Uppercase logo treatment: **IT'S A BUDGET**
- Machine-safe slug: `its-a-budget`
- Do not use `Its a Budget`, `Its-A-Budget`, or `It's a Budget` in new
  user-facing material.

The apostrophe is part of the product name. Use the straight apostrophe in
configuration and source text for consistent encoding. Typography may use a
curly apostrophe when it is deliberately typeset.

## Voice

It's a Budget is practical, reassuring, and plainspoken. It should feel useful
rather than judgmental. Prefer concrete language about planning, checkbook
balances, and upcoming bills. Avoid fear, shame, get-rich claims, and promises
that financial outcomes are guaranteed.

## Core Message

> Your money, finally in one place.

Supporting message:

> A checkbook-first way to plan ahead, track honestly, and stay in control.

Keep clear space around the logo. Do not stretch, recolor, rotate, add shadows,
or place text over the mark. On compact surfaces, use the mark with live text
rather than shrinking the full stacked logo until its words are unreadable.

Use purple for primary identity, teal for supporting emphasis, and magenta
sparingly. Maintain WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive controls.

## Typography

Use the application's system sans-serif stack for speed, privacy, and reliable
rendering:

```css
font-family: Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont,
  "Segoe UI", sans-serif;
```

The supplied raster logo remains the authority for the logo's letterforms.
Live headings should be bold and compact without attempting to reproduce the
logo exactly.
